Built for Liberia

The Liberia Online Library exists so that every Liberian — whether in Monrovia, Lofa, Maryland, or the diaspora — can access knowledge freely.

We focus on resources that matter here: our history, our languages, our farming knowledge, our children’s education, and the skills needed to build businesses and communities.

This library is designed to work on slow connections and simple phones. No heavy apps. No paywalls. Just knowledge.

  • 100% free forever
  • Works on 2G and low-end phones
  • Liberian history, culture & practical knowledge
  • Open for students, teachers, farmers & entrepreneurs

How to use this library

  1. Search or browse by category
  2. Open a book or resource
  3. Read online or download when available
  4. Share with friends and family

Many resources link to free open libraries (Project Gutenberg, Open Library, African Storybook, and others).
